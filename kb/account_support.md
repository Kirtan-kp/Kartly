# Account, membership, warranty and support

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Changing your email or phone
Email and phone number are changed in your account settings after a one-time verification code. We cannot change them through a support chat.

## Deleting your account or data
Account deletion and data requests are handled by our privacy team, not by the chat assistant. A human colleague will confirm your identity and process the request.

## Gold membership
Gold members get a 10-day return window, priority support and free delivery on every order. Membership tiers are Standard, Silver and Gold, based on past spend.

## Warranty on electronics
Electronics carry the brand's warranty, typically 6 months. The warranty card is in the box. Warranty repairs are handled by the brand's service centre, not by Kartly.

## Support hours
Support chat is staffed from 9 am to 9 pm IST every day. Outside those hours the assistant can answer and a colleague follows up the next morning.
