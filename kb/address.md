# Changing a delivery address

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## When the address can be changed
The delivery address can be changed only before the order ships. After it has shipped the courier already holds the old address and we cannot change it.

## Rules for a new address
The new address must be in India and in a pincode we deliver to. We confirm the pincode and the city match. Each order allows one address change.

## Fixing a wrong pincode or typo
A typo in the street, city or pincode is treated as an address change and follows the same rules. If the order has shipped and the courier cannot find you, ask them to call you; after three failed attempts the order is returned and refunded.

## Delivering to someone else
You can send an order to another person, such as a friend or family member, by giving their address and phone number. The order stays on your account and only you can ask about it.
