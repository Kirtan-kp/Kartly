# Payments and invoices

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, Kartly wallet and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery limit
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to INR 5,000. Orders above that amount must be paid online before dispatch.

## Failed payments and double charges
If money was deducted but the order failed, or you were charged twice, the extra amount is reversed to your account automatically within 5 to 7 business days. If it has not arrived after that, write to us with the transaction reference.

## GST invoices
A GST invoice is available on the order page once the order ships. If you need one with your business GST number, tell us the number before the order ships.

## Payments you do not recognise
If your card or account shows a payment to Kartly that you did not make, do not wait for it to be reversed. Our security team handles these cases and a human colleague will contact you.
