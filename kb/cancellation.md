# Cancelling an order

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Cancelling before shipping
An order can be cancelled free of charge until it ships. The cancellation takes effect immediately and the refund follows the normal refund timeline.

## Cancelling after shipping
Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the courier arrives, or accept it and return it under the return policy once it is delivered.

## Cancelling part of an order
Before shipping you can cancel a single item from an order with several items. The refund covers that item and its share of tax.

## How to cancel
Tell us the order number in the app or in a support message. We check that the order has not shipped and cancel it on the spot. Orders show "Packed" or "Shipped" once they are no longer cancellable.

## Cancelling a cash on delivery order
Cash on delivery orders can be cancelled before shipping at no charge. There is nothing to refund because no payment was taken.
