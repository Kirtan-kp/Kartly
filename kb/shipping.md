# Shipping and delivery

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on orders of INR 499 or more. Below that a delivery charge of INR 49 applies.

## Delivery times
Orders to the 25 metro cities usually arrive in 2 to 4 days. Other locations take 4 to 7 days. The estimated delivery date is shown on the order page once the order ships.

## Delivery days
We deliver Monday to Saturday to every serviceable pincode. Sunday delivery is available only in the 25 metro cities, which include Indore, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad.

## Tracking your order
Once shipped, the order page shows the courier name, the tracking number and the latest scan. Our carriers include Delhivery and BlueDart. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed orders
A parcel is delayed when it passes its estimated delivery date or the courier reports an exception such as an address problem or a hub delay. If your order is more than 3 days late, write to us and we will chase the courier and offer a reshipment or a refund.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel is returned to our warehouse and the order is refunded, minus delivery charges.

## Delivery locations
We deliver only within India. International delivery is not available.

## Parcel delivered but not received
If tracking says delivered but you have not received it, check with neighbours and building security for 24 hours, then write to us. We ask the courier for proof of delivery and will reship or refund if it cannot be found.
