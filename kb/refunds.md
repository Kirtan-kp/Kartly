# Refunds

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Refund timeline
Refunds are issued 5 to 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes the quality check. For orders cancelled before shipping, the refund starts immediately and takes 5 to 7 business days to show in your account.

## Where the money goes
Refunds go back to the original payment method: the card, UPI account or bank used at checkout. For cash on delivery orders we refund to a bank account you provide, or to Kartly wallet credit, within 7 to 10 days.

## Refund approval limits
Refunds up to INR 1,000 on an order are processed automatically once eligibility is confirmed. Refunds above INR 1,000 are reviewed by a human colleague, usually within 1 business day, before money is sent.

## What is included in a refund
The refund covers the price of the item and any tax paid. Delivery charges are refunded only when the order is cancelled before shipping or the item was defective, damaged or wrong. A return pickup fee, if charged, is deducted.

## Partial refunds
If an item arrives with a minor problem you may be offered a partial refund and keep the item. You can decline and return it for a full refund instead.

## Refund not received
If more than 7 business days have passed since your return was received, write to us with the order number. We check the refund status with the payment provider and share the reference number.

## Wallet credit
You can choose Kartly wallet credit instead of a refund to your bank. It is added instantly and can be used on any order. Wallet credit cannot be converted back to money.
