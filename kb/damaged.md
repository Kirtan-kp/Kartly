# Damaged, defective or wrong items

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Reporting a problem
Report a damaged, defective or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery. Send clear photos of the item and the packaging. After 48 hours the normal return rules apply.

## What we do
For a verified problem you choose a replacement or a full refund. Pickup of the faulty item is free and no return fee is charged.

## Missing items in a parcel
If an item is missing from a parcel, report it within 48 hours with a photo of the parcel and its label. We check with the warehouse and send the missing item or refund it.

## Damaged packaging
If the outer packaging is torn or wet when the courier arrives, you may refuse the delivery. Otherwise record the condition with photos and report it within 48 hours.
