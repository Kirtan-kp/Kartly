# Returns and exchanges

> Synthetic policy text for the Kartly demo store. Not a real company's policy.

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Gold members get 10 days. The window starts the day the carrier marks the parcel delivered, and the item must be unused, in its original packaging with all tags and accessories.

## Items that cannot be returned
Items marked "Final sale" at checkout cannot be returned or exchanged. Innerwear, opened personal-care products, gift cards and perishable goods are also not returnable, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.

## How to start a return
Tell us the order number and the item you want to send back, in the app or by writing to support. We check eligibility and create a return label. A courier collects the parcel from your address within 2 working days, or you can hand it to a partner drop-off point.

## Return shipping fee
Return pickup is free when the item is defective, damaged or not what you ordered. For any other reason a pickup fee of INR 60 is deducted from your refund.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within the same return window, subject to stock. If the new item is not available we refund the original instead.

## Items bought on sale
Sale items are returnable on the same terms as any other item, unless they are marked "Final sale".
